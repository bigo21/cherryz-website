# Accueil (/) — surface brief

Scope: home page of the public site, mode **Persuade**. Pilot of the "connectivity, image-rich" exploration on branch `refonte-mur` (version 1 of 3; `refonte-faisceaux` and `refonte-couverture` follow). Other pages follow once a version is chosen.
Audience: Cameroonian decision makers for both offers, equal weight (DG/DSI for Internet B2B; entrepreneurs, owners, investors for WiFi Zone). Often on mobile, sometimes on slow connections.
Job / action: pick a path within seconds, feel connectivity (bandwidth, coverage, high speed), see the broker logic on real figures, open the matching quote questionnaire or WhatsApp.
Proof on hand: operator partners, client logos, ART regulation, price ranges as labelled estimates. Photos are illustrative (no documented Cherryz work): captioned as such.
Constraints (user, 2026-10-30): image-rich, every photo of the original site used somewhere on the site (home uses most); the quote-book / ticket / perforated sheet / stamp world is rejected; animations with GSAP and Motion, scroll-scripted, content readable without them; the 220px `fond-*` photos only as small thumbnails; logo, cherry/navy, two offers, questionnaires and FR/EN unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a network operations wall where every photo is a client site going online in front of you. It refuses the operator default of one stock hero photo, a centred headline and a row of equal offer cards.

OWN-WORLD: A dark navy video wall (#0a121c seams, #101b28 tiles) of edge-to-edge photographs joined by 2px seams; every tile carries a status strip: link line (solid online, dashed negotiating, broken offline; never colour alone), site label, tabular readout in Mbps or ms. Cherry is the live accent: selected path, active link, primary action. Light cool panels (#e9eef3) for reading passages. Barlow Condensed display, Barlow body, a mono only for live measurements. No glow, no gradients-as-decoration, no cards.

STORY: The visitor sees connectivity happening across Cameroonian places, understands Cherryz sets operators in competition for them, believes the saving through the bandwidth console, and opens the questionnaire for their path.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height wall under the nav. Control tile (left 5/12, top 3 rows): H1, one line, path switch Entreprise / WiFi Zone, cherry "Recevoir mon devis" to the matching questionnaire. Photo tiles fill the rest (Yaoundé aerial largest); bottom row of five tiles. One caption line: photos and readouts are illustrative.

SIGNATURE: "Mise en ligne": tiles light up in a sweep from the control tile, grey to colour, dashed to solid link, readouts counting up; the path switch retunes the whole wall at once (tiles wipe to the other path's places). Scroll: operator feeds converge into Cherryz (drawn lines, scrubbed), the six field steps run as a pinned horizontal wall, coverage rings open over WiFi places. Off under reduced motion.
