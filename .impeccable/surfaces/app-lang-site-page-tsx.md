# Accueil (/) — surface brief

Scope: home page of the public site, mode **Persuade**. Pilot of the "connectivity, image-rich" exploration, version 2 of 3 on branch `refonte-faisceaux` (version 1: `refonte-mur`; version 3: `refonte-couverture`). Other pages follow once a version is chosen.
Audience: Cameroonian decision makers for both offers, equal weight (DG/DSI for Internet B2B; entrepreneurs, owners, investors for WiFi Zone). Often on mobile, sometimes on slow connections.
Job / action: pick a path within seconds, feel connectivity (bandwidth, coverage, high speed), see the broker logic on real figures, open the matching quote questionnaire or WhatsApp.
Proof on hand: operator partners, client logos, ART regulation, price ranges as labelled estimates. Photos are illustrative (no documented Cherryz work): captioned as such.
Constraints (user, 2026-10-30): image-rich, every photo of the original site used somewhere on the site (home uses most); the quote-book / ticket / perforated sheet / stamp world is rejected; animations with GSAP and Motion, scroll-scripted, content readable without them; the 220px `fond-*` photos only as small thumbnails; logo, cherry/navy, two offers, questionnaires and FR/EN unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Scrolling draws a microwave link from rooftop to rooftop over Yaoundé at dusk: the connection arrives without civil works, photo after photo. It refuses the operator default of one stock hero, a centred headline and a row of equal offer cards.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed dusk photography (Yaoundé aerial, residence at sunset) on a night ground (#0a111b, #152031). One cherry beam (#ff3d6a on photos, #a2032d on light ground) drawn as a line of sight with its dashed Fresnel zone; WiFi coverage as rings from a real rooftop antenna. Light warm reading ground (#f3f0eb) for the bandwidth passage. Barlow Condensed display, Barlow body, no mono. Square corners, no cards, no glow.

STORY: The visitor sees the link land on their building, follows the route operators → Cherryz → roof → site, sets their own bandwidth (beam thickness, price bars), sees the six field steps hung on one beam, then opens the questionnaire for their path.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height Yaoundé aerial at dusk under the transparent nav. Title on the sky, top left. A beam leaves the red-and-white mast and lands on the visitor's rooftop with a callout photo; controls panel bottom left (lead, Entreprise / WiFi Zone, cherry quote button, trust line). Caption: photos and figures illustrative.

SIGNATURE: The beam draws itself on arrival and re-aims at the other offer's building on path switch (Motion). Scroll: pinned route where the beam advances node by node while each leg's photo wipes in (GSAP scrub); the beam's thickness follows the chosen bandwidth (Motion springs); the field steps' beam fills down the page and opens each photo from its side; coverage rings spread from the rooftop antenna with scroll. Off under reduced motion; all content readable without JavaScript.

FORM: Line-of-sight link over a real city photograph; points pinned to the photo with a cover stage (aspect-ratio box in container units) so the beam stays on the same rooftops at every size.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; the chosen version ends with the finish review, the verdict and DESIGN.md.
